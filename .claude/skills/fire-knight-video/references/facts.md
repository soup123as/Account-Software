## The Ranger (product page): mining, industrial, vehicle
- "Industrial Fire Detection & Suppression" for vehicles, mining equipment (HEMM), industrial machinery, generators, enclosed electrical environments, engine bays.
- Detection technologies (five): PT100 temperature monitoring · Linear Heat Detection (LHD) cable that "detects heat along the entire cable length rather than at a single point" · Environmental Profiling (Standard / High Temp, 2 modes) · cylinder Pressure Monitoring with a Low Pressure LED · Manual Activation by a protected lift-cover button.
- How it works: Continuous Monitoring → Threat Identified (multi-sensor confirmation) → Alert Activated (audible alarm, emergency lighting, status LEDs show Triggered; "operators are alerted before suppression deploys") → Suppression Deploys (agent discharges automatically through the engineered nozzle system at the fire zone).
- Status indicators: Power · Armed · Triggered · Low Pressure · Fault.
- Marine-grade stainless steel components. Primary power plus battery backup. New build or retrofit.

## Claims guard
- Call the products "fire suppression aids". Never say: extinguishes, puts out any fire, 100%, guaranteed, replaces your extinguisher, patented, DGMS/AIS/BIS/NFPA approved.
- No response-time numbers or activation thresholds on screen.
- Label conceptual sequences `Conceptual illustration`.
- No named incidents, people, companies, competitors or third-party logos.
