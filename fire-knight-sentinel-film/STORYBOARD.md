# The Fire Knight · Bus Owners × The Sentinel — storyboard

## PAGE
- **Problem:** bus owners think a red extinguisher behind the driver's seat is their fire safety. Fires start in the engine bay and battery, where nobody is looking.
- **Audience:** bus and coach fleet owners in India, on Instagram, often muted, mid-scroll.
- **Goal:** comment ("kitne zone covered hain?"), then enquire.
- **Emotion:** from "we are covered" to an uneasy "nobody is watching back there" to relief that something is.

One true thing: the Sentinel watches each zone with its own IR flame sensor and, in Normal Mode, discharges only the zone that triggers (site: "Detect / Decide / Discharge / Notify").

## Ideas
1. (first, put aside) X-ray bus with three highlighted zones. It is the category's film; any vehicle-suppression brand could run it. Already made twice for this client.
2. **Nazar: who is looking where.** The driver's mirror watches the passengers; nothing watches the engine; the Sentinel's lens is the eye that does. Kind: a held world with one carried motif (the eye / lens).
3. Stillness that breaks: one locked shot of the dark engine bay for the whole film.

**Chosen: 2.** The lens-as-eye is this product's own part and the script's own verb ("nazar"). Cover the logo and the steel oval with one black lens still says whose it is; a competitor's nozzle head does not look back at you. Idea 3 is folded in as the world of the middle act, not as a second idea.

Accent: **gold = the Sentinel acting** (its name, its watch, its discharge word). Red is only the real extinguisher, the real fire and the real alert.

## World (one logic for the whole film)
- **Ground:** inside and around one coach at night. Near-black steel, never a flat swatch.
- **Light:** sodium street lamps pass outside, so a warm band of light sweeps every shot every ~1.7 s (the bus is moving; no dead frame). Other light only from sources: mirror glow, the fire, the phone, the open door.
- **Depth / texture:** grain over everything; shots are close on objects, cropped by the frame.
- **Hero moment (screenshot):** macro in the dark engine bay: the steel sensor module turns to face the camera like an eye opening, the passing light rakes its brushed face, the fire reflects in its lens. Built in real 3D (hero3d).
- **Picture sources:** the coach, cabin, engine bay, battery, dashboard are illustrations from this project's own drawings; the Sentinel system shot is the real render from thefireknight.in; the 3D module is a rebuild of the real part; the phone is native-ui with an illustrative notification.
- **Sound:** no ElevenLabs key, so no generated voice or music. A synthesised bed (drone, road rumble, 3 placed hits, hiss, chime) from this project's own synth; the client's voiceover drops in later.
